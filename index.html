My Rogerian Argument

A five-step path from disagreement to common ground

1

Define the Problem

Clearly explain the issue or disagreement.

2

Acknowledge the Other Side

Present the opposing position fairly and respectfully.

3

State Your Position

Explain your position without dismissing the other side.

4

Bring Two Sides Together

Identify shared values, common ground, or possible compromise.

5

Reach a Conclusion

Propose a fair resolution or productive next step.

Rogerian arguments seek understanding, accurately represent opposing views, and build toward a mutually acceptable outcome.
